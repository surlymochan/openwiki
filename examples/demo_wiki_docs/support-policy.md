# Support Policy

## On-Call Handoff

The on-call handoff should happen every Monday at 10:00.

## Escalation

- P1 incidents page the primary and secondary on-call engineers.
- P2 incidents notify the team channel and issue owner.

## Support Principles

- Customer-facing incidents should be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
- If an incident spans multiple services, the incident commander coordinates the shared timeline.
